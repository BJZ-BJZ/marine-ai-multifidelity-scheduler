# Rights and data scope

This edition does not add an open-source licence on behalf of an unconfirmed author or grant rights to third-party data. Repository visibility and a licence choice are separate decisions. Before adding a licence, the owner should confirm ownership of the research-generated code, frozen models and simulation artifacts.

Included: portable code adaptations, research-generated numerical arrays, frozen parameters, route-segment statistical aggregates, and collapsed state-scoring counts.

Excluded: original AIS tracks, complete third-party datasets, proprietary trial logs, third-party paper PDFs, paper submission drafts, private conversations, credentials, author identity documents, and native solver binaries.

Raw data regeneration or redistribution follows the original providers' terms. A SHA-256 provenance record does not grant redistribution permission.

The drilling project additionally includes the Utah FORGE processed Pason dataset under CC BY 4.0 and identified research-derived adaptations. See projects/drilling-p26-194/DATA_ATTRIBUTION.md. The general repository note does not override that dataset licence.
